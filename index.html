Our Story

서로 다른 길, 하나의 여정.
Different paths, one journey.
Diferentes caminos, un mismo destino.

As many of you know, Keily and Nathan worked at the same company, Skyworks. What you might not know is how easily they could have missed each other. They worked in different buildings, living separate days just a short distance apart. Then a pickleball group formed at work, and their paths finally crossed.

At first, they simply knew of each other. But fate had another meeting in mind. One semester, they found themselves in the same class, pursuing the same master’s program at the same university. Keily couldn’t help noticing Nathan: confident, outgoing, and passionate about his education. He could make the entire class laugh with one of his clever jokes and, a moment later, show everyone just how brilliant he was. Nathan noticed Keily’s smile. He says she had a way of lighting up every room she entered.

Soon, there were assignments to work on together and pickleball games to play. They began looking forward to seeing each other, and somewhere along the way, friendship turned into love. Many of you were there as their worlds grew closer, sharing in the family gatherings, celebrations, and little moments that made their story theirs.

And then Nathan planned a surprise. Keily arrived at what she believed was a party for her parents’ anniversary. Instead, she walked in to a mariachi playing “Hermoso Cariño.” Surrounded by friends and family, she discovered what everyone else had been keeping secret: Nathan was about to ask her to marry him.

Now they get to gather with all of you again—this time to begin their next chapter as husband and wife. They can’t wait to celebrate with the people who have been part of their story all along.
